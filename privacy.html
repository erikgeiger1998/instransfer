Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 27, 2026

1. Overview

At GG Production, we prioritize your privacy. The Instransfer application is designed with a "Zero Cloud, Zero Trace" architecture. This means we do not collect, store, or transmit your personal data, files, or metadata to any external servers or databases.

2. How Your Files Are Handled

Instransfer operates entirely via direct Peer-to-Peer (P2P) connections using local networks (mDNS) and WebRTC. When you send a file to another user, the data is encrypted end-to-end (E2EE) and transmitted directly to their device. We do not have access to your files, nor do we store them in any cloud storage or database.

3. Data Collection

We do not collect any personal information, analytics, or usage data. The app requires certain device permissions (such as Local Network Access and Camera for QR code scanning) strictly to facilitate the direct file transfer between you and your peers.

4. Third-Party Services

We do not integrate with third-party tracking or advertising services. Your activity within the app remains strictly on your device.
